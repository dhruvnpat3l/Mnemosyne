# Mnemosyne Privacy Policy

**Published by Zendacros**  
**Last updated: 18 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Mnemosyne, an Android book-reading application published by Zendacros (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles your information.

Mnemosyne works without an account and processes your library on your device. We do not receive your books or reading activity through the app. Android system backups may store app data separately, as explained below.

## 1. Information stored on your device

When you import or open an EPUB file, Mnemosyne reads the file you select and stores a private copy on your device.

Mnemosyne also stores the information needed to maintain your library and reading experience, including:

- Book content and metadata, such as titles, authors, covers, source file references, and library categories.
- Reading positions, reading progress, and recently opened books.
- Bookmarks and highlights.
- Reading and display preferences.

This information is stored locally in the app’s private storage, subject to the Android backup and device-transfer features described in Section 4.

## 2. How your information is used

Mnemosyne uses locally stored information to display and organize your library, render book pages, resume reading where you left off, and save your annotations and preferences.

The built-in dictionary uses data included with the app to look up words.

Mnemosyne does not require access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

## 3. Network access and third parties

Mnemosyne does not include advertising, analytics, or a service that uploads your books or reading activity to Zendacros. We do not receive or sell your library data through the app.

If you select a book through a cloud storage service or another file provider, that provider handles access to the original file under its own privacy policy.

If you open a link within a book, the link opens in another application, such as your browser. Those applications and websites handle information under their own privacy policies.

## 4. Android backups and device transfer

Android backup is enabled for Mnemosyne. Depending on your device, Android version, backup settings, and system limits, app files, library records, annotations, and preferences may be copied to your backup provider or transferred to another device.

These backups and transfers are managed by Android and your backup provider, rather than by Zendacros. You can review your device’s backup settings to manage available backup options.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Mnemosyne retains locally stored data until you remove it, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app.

Deleting a book removes its library records and annotations. Mnemosyne also attempts to delete its private imported files and reports if file cleanup fails. Deleting a book from Mnemosyne does not change or delete your original EPUB file.

You can remove the app’s local data by clearing Mnemosyne’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Original files stored elsewhere and existing system backups are managed separately. Restoring a system backup may restore previously backed-up Mnemosyne data.

## 6. Storage and security

Mnemosyne stores your library in app-private storage protected by Android’s app isolation features.

The protection of your information also depends on your device security and backup settings. No storage method can guarantee complete protection against unauthorized access or data loss.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Mnemosyne and its data-handling practices. Updates will appear on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

## 8. Contact

For questions about Mnemosyne or this Privacy Policy, contact Zendacros using the developer contact details provided on Mnemosyne’s app store listing.
